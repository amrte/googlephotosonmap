Your Google Photos, placed on the map

Google's own apps are the only ones allowed to read your photo locations, so Photo Atlas works from a Google Takeout export: a copy of your library that Google packs up for you, locations included.

  1. Request the export at takeout.google.com.Click "Deselect all", tick only Google Photos, then "Next step". Pick the largest file size (50 GB) so you get fewer files.
  2. Download the ZIP files when Google emails you.That takes minutes to days depending on library size. No need to unzip them. If you chose "Add to Drive" as the destination, use "From Google Drive" below instead.
  3. Add all the ZIPs here at once, or drop them onto this page.An unzipped Takeout folder works too, and so does any folder of photos with GPS data.

Nothing is uploaded. Your browser reads the files on this computer; only the map background comes from the internet. If Chrome asks whether to "upload" a folder, that is its standard wording for letting a page read it.
